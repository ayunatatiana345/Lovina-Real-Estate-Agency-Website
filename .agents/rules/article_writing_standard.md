# MASTER ARTICLE WRITING STANDARD — PT LOVINA NORTH BALI REAL ESTATE AGENCY

Use this writing standard whenever creating, revising, expanding, or improving article content for the public website.

The goal is NOT simply to make the article "SEO-friendly".

The goal is to create content that is:
- accurate
- useful
- informative
- credible
- easy to understand
- natural to read
- professionally written
- locally relevant
- genuinely helpful to property buyers, homeowners, renters, and investors
- SEO-friendly in a natural way
- substantial enough to provide real value

The article should feel like a knowledgeable real estate professional wrote it for real people, NOT like AI-generated SEO content.

==================================================
1. WRITING STYLE
==================================================

Use clear, natural, professional English.

The writing should be easy to understand for an international audience, including readers who may not be highly familiar with Bali's property market.

Use straightforward sentences.

Avoid unnecessarily complicated vocabulary.

Avoid academic or overly formal language unless the subject genuinely requires it.

Avoid sentences that sound artificially sophisticated.

Prefer:
"Buyers should check the property's access road before making a decision."

instead of overly complicated wording such as:
"Prospective purchasers are strongly advised to undertake a comprehensive evaluation of vehicular accessibility prior to proceeding with the acquisition."

Keep the language professional, but human.

==================================================
2. NATURAL HUMAN WRITING
==================================================

The article must NOT sound like generic AI content.

Avoid repetitive patterns such as:
- "It is important to..."
- "Furthermore..."
- "Moreover..."
- "In today's..."
- "When it comes to..."
- "Whether you are..."
- "This comprehensive guide..."
- "In conclusion..."
- "By understanding..."
- "By taking the time to..."

These phrases may be used when genuinely appropriate, but DO NOT repeatedly use the same sentence patterns throughout the article.

Vary sentence length and paragraph structure naturally.
Some paragraphs can be short.
Some can be slightly longer.

Do not make every section follow exactly the same formula.
The writing should feel naturally edited by a human.

==================================================
3. EASY TO UNDERSTAND
==================================================

Prioritize clarity over impressive vocabulary.

Explain specialized terms when they first appear.

For example:
"Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung (PBG) is the current building approval framework in Indonesia."

Do not assume every international reader already understands Indonesian property terminology.

When using local terms such as:
- Buleleng
- Lovina
- Kalibukbuk
- Kaliasem
- Anturan
- Banjar
- Temukus
- SHM
- HGB
- PBG
- SLF
- RTRW
- RDTR

use them naturally and provide enough context for a reader to understand why they matter.

==================================================
4. INFORMATION MUST HAVE PURPOSE
==================================================

Every paragraph should contribute something useful.

Do not add sentences simply to increase word count.

Each section should answer one or more practical questions:
- What is this?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it affect a buyer?
- What should the reader check?
- What should the reader compare?
- What could go wrong?
- What should the reader consider before deciding?

Avoid filler.
Do not repeat the same point using different wording just to make the article longer.

==================================================
5. COMPLETE AND SUBSTANTIAL
==================================================

Articles should provide enough depth to genuinely answer the topic.

Do not make an article artificially short.
However, do NOT add unnecessary information merely to reach a word count.

A longer article should become more useful, not more repetitive.

Use a logical progression such as:
Introduction
→ Core topic
→ Practical considerations
→ Local context
→ Risks / limitations
→ Practical comparison or checklist
→ Final guidance
→ Relevant CTA

The exact structure may change depending on the article topic.

==================================================
6. SEO — NATURAL, NOT FORCED
==================================================

SEO is important, but readability comes first.

Use relevant keywords naturally based on the actual topic.
For example, depending on the article:
- Bali property
- North Bali property
- property in Bali
- North Bali real estate
- villa in North Bali
- Bali property investment
- property for sale in North Bali
- Lovina property
- Buleleng property
- property investment in Bali

DO NOT force keywords into every paragraph.
DO NOT repeat the same keyword unnaturally.
DO NOT create sentences that exist only to contain keywords.
Do not use keyword stuffing.

Use related terms, natural variations, and contextual language.
Google should be able to understand the topic from the overall content, structure, headings, entities, and context — not from repetitive keyword insertion.

==================================================
7. SEARCH INTENT
==================================================

Before writing, understand what the reader is actually searching for.

For example:
- "How to Choose the Right Property in Bali" should answer:
  - How should buyers compare properties?
  - What should they look at?
  - What costs should they consider?
  - What legal checks matter?
  - How can they compare different properties?

- "Things to Consider Before Investing in Bali Property" should answer:
  - Is there a real market?
  - What costs affect returns?
  - What operational issues exist?
  - What risks should investors consider?
  - How should projections be stress-tested?
  - What should happen before committing capital?

- "Top Areas in North Bali for Villa Investment" should answer:
  - What are the relevant North Bali areas?
  - How are they different?
  - What is the character of each area?
  - What type of property or lifestyle might suit each area?
  - What should buyers verify at the individual property level?

The article must satisfy the reader's actual intent.

==================================================
8. FACTUAL ACCURACY
==================================================

Accuracy is MORE IMPORTANT than making the article sound impressive.

Never invent:
- statistics
- rental yields
- occupancy rates
- property appreciation percentages
- market growth percentages
- tourism numbers
- exact prices
- exact travel times
- claims about demand
- claims about profitability
- claims about "best" locations
- claims about guaranteed returns
- legal rules that have not been verified

If a number is not supported by a reliable source, do not present it as a fact.
If an example is useful, clearly label it as an illustrative example or modelling assumption.

For example:
"An illustrative occupancy assumption of 60% can be used for a basic financial model."
Do NOT write:
"North Bali villas normally achieve 60% occupancy."
unless reliable evidence supports that claim.

==================================================
9. AVOID ABSOLUTE CLAIMS
==================================================

Real estate varies significantly by property.

Avoid statements such as:
- "Lovina is the best place to invest."
- "Banjar is guaranteed to perform well."
- "This area has the highest rental demand."
- "This property type always generates strong returns."

Instead use accurate, conditional language:
- "Lovina may suit buyers looking for..."
- "Banjar may appeal to buyers who prioritize..."
- "Properties in this area can be considered for..."
- "The suitability depends on the property's exact location, condition, access, documentation, and intended use."

This makes the article more credible.

==================================================
10. LOCAL ACCURACY
==================================================

When discussing North Bali, distinguish between:
- a village
- a district/subdistrict
- a tourism area
- a broader locality
- Buleleng Regency

Do not treat every place name as if it represents the same geographic level.
For example, Lovina should be described appropriately as a coastal tourism area that includes several surrounding villages.
When discussing Banjar, make the context clear when necessary because "Banjar" can refer to different geographic or local contexts.
Use local place names accurately.
Do not invent addresses or geographic relationships.

==================================================
11. LEGAL AND INVESTMENT INFORMATION
==================================================

Legal and financial topics require extra caution.
Do not present the article as legal, tax, or financial advice.
Do not oversimplify Indonesian property ownership structures.

When discussing:
- SHM
- HGB
- Hak Sewa
- Hak Pakai
- PMA
- PBG
- SLF
- zoning
- land use
- taxes
- transaction costs

explain them carefully and avoid implying that one structure automatically applies to every buyer.
Where appropriate, tell readers to confirm the specific situation with qualified legal, tax, or property professionals.
The article should educate readers without pretending to replace professional due diligence.

==================================================
12. INVESTMENT CONTENT
==================================================

Investment articles should be analytical rather than promotional.

Discuss:
- demand
- location
- operating costs
- management
- maintenance
- accessibility
- legal documentation
- marketability
- risk
- exit considerations

Do not promise:
- guaranteed ROI
- guaranteed rental income
- guaranteed appreciation
- guaranteed occupancy
- "high-yield" results without evidence

The reader should finish the article understanding both opportunities AND risks.

==================================================
13. LOCAL PROPERTY CONTEXT
==================================================

When appropriate, connect general property advice to the actual North Bali context.

Relevant areas may include:
- Lovina
- Kalibukbuk
- Kaliasem
- Anturan
- Banjar
- Temukus
- Seririt
- Singaraja
- Gerokgak
- Pemuteran
- Kayuputih
- Munduk

However:
DO NOT force every location into every article.
Only mention an area when it genuinely contributes to the topic.
Do not create artificial keyword lists.

==================================================
14. STRUCTURE AND HEADINGS
==================================================

Use descriptive headings that tell the reader what the section is about.

Good:
- "Look at the Total Cost, Not Only the Asking Price"
- "Do Not Skip Property and Legal Checks"
- "How Much Management Will the Property Require?"

Avoid vague headings such as:
- "Important Things"
- "Other Information"
- "Final Information"

Use H1 only for the main article title.
Use H2 for major sections.
Use H3 when a major section genuinely needs subsections.
Do not create excessive heading levels.

==================================================
15. PARAGRAPH STYLE
==================================================

Keep paragraphs reasonably short.
Avoid huge blocks of text.
Generally aim for approximately 2–5 sentences per paragraph when appropriate.
Use bullet points when they genuinely improve readability.
Do not turn everything into bullet points.
Use tables only when a comparison is genuinely easier to understand in table form.

==================================================
16. PRACTICAL VALUE
==================================================

Whenever possible, give the reader something they can actually do.

Examples:
- comparison checklist
- questions to ask
- factors to inspect
- simple scoring matrix
- financial modelling framework
- due-diligence checklist
- location comparison
- decision-making framework

The reader should be able to use the article in a real property search.

==================================================
17. INTERNAL LINKS
==================================================

Use internal links naturally when relevant.

Examples:
- Properties
- Locations
- Contact Us
- related property articles

Do not add links simply for SEO.
Anchor text should clearly describe the destination.
Avoid excessive internal links.

==================================================
18. CTA WRITING
==================================================

The CTA should feel like a natural continuation of the article.
Do not suddenly switch into aggressive sales language.

Good CTA:
"If you are exploring property opportunities in North Bali, you can view our current listings or contact our team to discuss your preferred location, property type, and requirements."

Avoid:
- "Don't miss this amazing opportunity!"
- "Invest now before prices go up!"
- "Contact us today for guaranteed high returns!"

The CTA should be professional, informative, and credible.

==================================================
19. DO NOT WRITE FOR SEARCH ENGINES ALONE
==================================================

The primary reader is a real person.
Write content that a potential property buyer would actually want to read.

If a sentence improves SEO but makes the article awkward, rewrite the sentence naturally.
If a keyword does not fit naturally, do not force it.
If removing a repetitive keyword makes the article easier to read, remove it.

==================================================
20. FINAL QUALITY CHECK
==================================================

Before finalizing any article, check:

CONTENT:
[ ] Is the article genuinely informative?
[ ] Does every section have a clear purpose?
[ ] Is the topic covered thoroughly?
[ ] Is there useful practical advice?
[ ] Is the information understandable to an international reader?

ACCURACY:
[ ] Are locations described correctly?
[ ] Are legal terms handled carefully?
[ ] Are unsupported claims removed?
[ ] Are statistics/numbers supported or clearly labelled as examples?
[ ] Are investment claims appropriately qualified?
[ ] Are there no guarantees or exaggerated claims?

WRITING:
[ ] Does it sound natural?
[ ] Does it sound human?
[ ] Is the English easy to understand?
[ ] Are sentences varied naturally?
[ ] Are paragraphs easy to read?
[ ] Is unnecessary repetition removed?
[ ] Does it avoid generic AI-style wording?

SEO:
[ ] Is the main topic obvious?
[ ] Are relevant keywords used naturally?
[ ] Are related terms used naturally?
[ ] Is there no keyword stuffing?
[ ] Are headings descriptive and useful?
[ ] Does the article satisfy search intent?
[ ] Are relevant internal links included naturally?

USER VALUE:
[ ] Would a real buyer learn something useful from this?
[ ] Does the article help the reader make a better decision?
[ ] Does it explain both benefits and risks?
[ ] Does it avoid making unrealistic promises?

==================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE:
==================================================

WRITE FOR PEOPLE FIRST.
OPTIMIZE FOR SEARCH ENGINES NATURALLY.

The article should be:
ACCURATE + CLEAR + COMPLETE + USEFUL + CREDIBLE + NATURAL + SEO-FRIENDLY.

Never sacrifice accuracy or readability just to insert keywords.
